Beyond The Camera

My World

At 1.99 m I am usually the tallest person in the room, but what people remember is what I bring with me: manga under my arm, a new word in another language, and the energy of someone who trains every day. These are the things that shape how I move, how I pose and how I tell a story in front of the lens.

01 — Anime & Manga

Characters taught me how to pose

I grew up with Naruto, Bleach, Gintama and Slam Dunk. What fascinates me is how a mangaka can tell everything with one drawing: a coat caught in the wind, a half-smile, a hand in a pocket.

That is what I try to bring on set: a clear attitude, a silhouette you can read at a glance, and a touch of playfulness. Cosplay, drawing my favourite characters and streaming games are part of the same passion.

  • Bleach
  • Naruto
  • Jujutsu Kaisen
  • Gintama
  • Cosplay
  • Gaming
02 — Languages

Seven languages, one curiosity

French is my mother tongue. I also speak English, Polish, Mandarin Chinese, German, Spanish and Japanese, and I am starting Korean.

Every language changes the way you joke, listen and look at people. On a shoot, it means I can work easily with international teams, and travel has given me the habit of feeling at home almost anywhere.

  • Français
  • English
  • Polski
  • 中文
  • Deutsch
  • Español
  • 日本語
  • 한국어
03 — Sport & Martial Arts

Discipline you can see in a pose

Boxing, MMA, nunchaku, basketball, sprinting and swimming: I need movement in my life.

Martial arts in particular taught me balance, control and how to hold a position with intention. Those are exactly the skills a photographer needs from a model, whether it is a still portrait or an action shot for a sports brand.

  • Boxing
  • MMA
  • Nunchaku
  • Basketball
  • Sprint
  • Swimming
04 — Health & Fitness

My body is part of the job

My mornings start with a journal, some reading and a short HIIT session. I train with consistency, eat well and protect my sleep.

Long hikes and time outdoors keep me balanced. For a brand, it means you work with someone reliable, energetic and camera-ready.

  • HIIT
  • Strength
  • Hiking
  • Routine
  • Sleep
05 — Art

I see the world in frames

Before modelling, I studied art. I still draw and paint, and I spend a lot of time in museums looking at how classical painters and sculptors placed light and bodies.

That eye for composition helps me understand what a photographer is building, and it is why I love street art, festivals of light and any place where colour becomes a scene.

  • Drawing
  • Painting
  • Museums
  • Street art
  • Light

Behind The Frames

The Stories

A picture is never only a picture. Here is why I chose some of these images and where the inspiration came from.

The Uniform

Green blazer, collar up

Inspired by the school uniforms of the anime I grew up with. Pulling the collar up turned a simple blazer into a shōnen hero entrance: theatrical, a bit cheeky, very me.

The Honest One

Black and white, no filter

Every model needs one picture with nothing to hide behind. White t-shirt, grey wall, natural hair: this is the face a brand will actually work with.

The Colour

Standing inside the light

I love places where light becomes art. Here I let the installation do the work and kept the pose still, the way a character stands in a key frame.

The City

New York behind me

My year in the United States taught me to feel at home anywhere. A plaid overshirt, the wind and the skyline: the image of a new chapter.

The Discipline

In the dojo

Martial arts are where I learned to control my body. That control is what lets me hold a pose, repeat a movement and stay precise on set.

The Silhouette

Backlit under an archway

Sometimes the story is told by the light, not the face. Like a manga panel in black and white, the shape alone says where the character is going.
